\title{ElevenLabs Catalog Index: A Z Specification}
\author{Punt Labs}
\maketitle

Scopes are credential digests; entries hold no client. The strong index
retains up to eight entries in oldest-first order. The weak index also
tracks entries that a caller holds after eviction. Isolated lookups and raw
IDs do not enter either index.

\begin{zed}
  [SCOPE, ENTRY]
\end{zed}

\begin{schema}{CatalogIndex}
  recent : \iseq SCOPE \\
  held : SCOPE \pfun \nat_1 \\
  live : SCOPE \pinj ENTRY
\where
  \# recent \leq 8 \\
  \dom live = \ran recent \cup \dom held
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{InitCatalog}
  CatalogIndex'
\where
  recent' = \langle \rangle \\
  held' = \emptyset \\
  live' = \emptyset
\end{schema}

Opening a lookup is atomic under the index lock. An existing live entry is
reused even if it is absent from the strong index. The caller's reference is
held while it waits for or owns the entry lock.

\begin{schema}{OpenLookup}
  \Delta CatalogIndex \\
  scope? : SCOPE \\
  entry! : ENTRY
\where
  scope? \in \dom live \implies entry! = live~scope? \\
  scope? \notin \dom live \implies entry! \notin \ran live \\
  scope? \in \dom held \implies
    held' = held \oplus \{scope? \mapsto held~scope? + 1\} \\
  scope? \notin \dom held \implies
    held' = held \cup \{scope? \mapsto 1\} \\
  \exists ordered : \iseq SCOPE @ \\
  \t1 ordered = (recent \filter (\ran recent \setminus \{scope?\}))
    \cat \langle scope? \rangle \land \\
  \t1 (\# ordered \leq 8 \implies recent' = ordered) \land \\
  \t1 (\# ordered > 8 \implies recent' = tail~ordered) \\
  live' = (\ran recent' \cup \dom held') \dres
    (live \oplus \{scope? \mapsto entry!\})
\end{schema}

Finishing a lookup, including on error, releases its reference. An evicted
entry disappears only once no caller still holds it.

\begin{schema}{CloseLookup}
  \Delta CatalogIndex \\
  scope? : SCOPE
\where
  recent' = recent \\
  scope? \in \dom held \\
  held~scope? = 1 \implies held' = \{scope?\} \ndres held \\
  held~scope? > 1 \implies
    held' = held \oplus \{scope? \mapsto held~scope? - 1\} \\
  live' = (\ran recent' \cup \dom held') \dres live
\end{schema}

The eviction-order and active-entry tests check the strong capacity and the
live identity invariant. The entry tests check single-fetch coordination and
release on refresh failure.
